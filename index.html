About Us


The Septimus Corporation, previously known as Septimus LLC, was founded in 1783 and now serves as a parent company for smaller businesses.  The Septimus Corporation currently maintains offices in New York, Chicago, Los Angeles, Paris, London, Tokyo, Moscow, Mumbai, Cairo, and Shanghai.
